Research introduction

A prediction means little until biology confirms it. I want to build methods that show their uncertainty and explain themselves, and test them in both dry and wet labs. My background spans microbiology benches, field work with farmers, and self-taught machine learning, and I am drawn to questions where those meet, such as climate-linked health and stress responses in plants and microbes.
